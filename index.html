Do you supply packing materials?

Yes. We can pack everything for you, or supply boxes, tape and paper so you can pack yourself. Materials are added to your quote, so you know the cost before you book.

Will you take furniture apart and put it back together?

Yes. Our team can dismantle beds, wardrobes and tables before the move and rebuild them in your new home. Mention it when you ask for a quote.

How much will my move cost?

Every move is quoted individually, based on how much you're moving, how far, and any extras such as packing or storage. Send us the details and we'll give you a clear price.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel 7 or more days before your move and it's free. We return your deposit in full.

Are my belongings insured?

Yes. Every move is covered by goods in transit and public liability insurance.

Can you move me at short notice?

Often, yes. We have same-day availability in many areas. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what we can do.
